Every payment has a checkout URL. Send your customer there. The page handles wallet selection, deep links and payment status.

## What the customer sees

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/hosted-checkout.jpg" alt="The hosted checkout on a phone: amount, countdown and wallet list" width="320" />
</Frame>

1. The amount in USDC and a countdown to the payment's `expiresAt`.
2. A list of wallets. On Stellar today: Lobstr, Beans and Albedo. Phantom is available for customers paying from Solana, with the funds bridged to your Stellar wallet.
3. Tapping a wallet opens it with the payment pre-filled. On a phone, if the wallet is not installed, the checkout shows the app store link instead.
4. The customer approves in their wallet. The checkout polls until the funds arrive, then shows **Paid** and sends the customer to your `returnUrl` if you set one.

## Opening the checkout

Redirect to `paymentCheckoutUrl`, or open it in a new tab. On mobile apps, open it in an in-app browser so the wallet deep links work. The [Flutter SDK](/sdks/flutter) does this for you.

## Return URL

`returnUrl` is where the checkout sends the customer after paying. It must be `http` or `https`. The customer can also close the tab and pay later from their wallet as long as the payment has not expired, so treat a visit to your return page as "they came back", not "they paid". The webhook is the source of truth for fulfilment.

## Expiry

Every payment has an `expiresAt`. After that time it moves to `Expired` and the checkout stops accepting funds. Create a new payment rather than reusing an old URL. Cross-chain payments that have started bridging never expire, since the funds are already in flight.
