Sandbox and mainnet are separate environments. Apps, keys, wallets and webhook secrets do not carry over, and sandbox payments never appear in production. Your integration code does not change.

## Before you switch

- Keep the base URL, secret key and webhook secret in environment variables, so the switch is a configuration change.
- Your settlement wallet must hold the mainnet USDC trustline. Add and verify it in the production dashboard the same way as on sandbox.
- Make sure your webhook endpoint is reachable from the internet over `https`, verifies the signature, deduplicates on the event id and fulfils only on `payment.paid`. The [webhooks guide](/guides/webhooks) covers all four.
- Make one small real payment end to end before you open it to customers.

## Good to know

There is no refund endpoint. A refund is a USDC transfer from your wallet to the customer's. Keep your sandbox app for testing changes before you ship them. Fees are on [paywit.xyz/pricing](https://paywit.xyz/pricing).
