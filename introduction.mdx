Paywit is a stablecoin checkout. Your customer pays in USDC from their own wallet to your wallet, and a signed webhook tells your backend when it is paid. Paywit does not hold the funds.

## How a payment works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your server creates a payment">
    One call to the API with the amount and your order id. You get back a hosted checkout URL.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The customer pays from their wallet">
    You send them to the checkout. They pick a wallet, approve the payment, and the USDC moves from their wallet to your settlement wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paywit confirms it on-chain">
    Paywit marks the payment paid when the funds arrive in your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your server gets a webhook">
    A signed `payment.paid` event with your order id. You fulfil the order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The integration is one call out and one webhook in. SDKs are available for [JavaScript](/sdks/javascript) and [Flutter](/sdks/flutter).
